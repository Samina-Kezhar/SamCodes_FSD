About Us

At Sweet Slice, we believe a truly great cheesecake takes time,
care, and the finest ingredients — no shortcuts. What started as a
love for baking has grown into a cozy neighborhood spot where
every slice is made fresh, from our classic New York style to rich
chocolate, bright strawberry, and seasonal specials that keep
things interesting.
We use real cream cheese, real fruit, and simple, honest recipes —
because we think the best desserts don't need anything fancy to
shine, just great flavor and a lot of love. Whether you're stopping in
for a quick treat with your coffee, picking up a whole cake for a
celebration, or just craving something sweet after a long day, we're
here to make your visit feel like a little escape.
Come say hi, grab a slice, and taste why our regulars keep coming
back for more..
